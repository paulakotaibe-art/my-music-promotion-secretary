# Manual support and data-request procedure

Operator: Paul Ifeanyi Akotaibe. Public support/privacy address, confirmed by owner 25 October 2026: hello@drpaulslibrary.com.

The /privacy and /data-deletion pages describe a manual request route, not an automatic erasure API. No records were deleted or email sent by this change.

When a request arrives:
1. Record scope/date, verify the registered account proportionately and acknowledge it. Do not request secrets or identity documents unless justified.
2. Inspect own account's queued jobs. Lock and stop queued work using administrative controls; if a worker already claimed a job, check its actual outcome before deleting its connection. Do not resend an uncertain publication.
3. Check cancellation separately. Do not equate deleting account data with cancelling Stripe renewal. Confirm any requested cancellation through the existing billing flow.
4. Review account, drafts, public album pages, uploaded storage objects, social connections, subscriptions and receipts. Identify data still needed for an unresolved dispute, payment/account security or legal duty; minimize it and record why. Delete/pseudonymize only after checking foreign keys and actual storage/provider deletion effects. A request for a single connection is not authority to erase everything.
5. Explain and confirm what was removed, retained or outside app control. Remove public file access where the verified scope requires file deletion. A database draft deletion alone does not remove the public storage object. No automatic backup-erasure or public social-post erasure should be promised.
6. Aim for a response within one calendar month, communicate any lawful extension, and keep only the minimal request record needed to evidence its handling. Review any retained exception when its purpose ends.

Retention criteria published: saved account/content while needed for the service the account holder requests; credentials while an authorized connection/scheduled work needs them; receipts while needed for result verification, duplicate protection and allowances; billing/security/request records only for the relevant unresolved purpose or legal duty. No fixed automatic inactivity purge is claimed. Before broader paid launch, complete the provider/processing-location/transfer review and any remaining statutory business and checkout disclosures. Public policy pages do not themselves establish Meta approval or legal certification.

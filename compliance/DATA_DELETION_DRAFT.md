# Data deletion instructions — review draft

Not active instructions until the public contact and working handling process are completed.

1. Contact [PUBLIC PRIVACY EMAIL REQUIRED] from your registered email. State that you want your Music Promotion Secretary account or connected-platform data deleted and identify the scope: connection, stored media, drafts, or account. Do not send passwords, email sign-in links, platform tokens, API keys or full payment details.
2. The operator verifies the request, checks queued work and subscriptions, and explains what will be deleted or retained and why. The app currently has no general self-service account deletion button or automatic cancellation of queued Facebook jobs; neither action should be claimed as implemented.
3. Before deleting connection data, stop or resolve queued jobs through an appropriate administrative process so removal does not create an uncertain publication result. Subscription renewal cancellation and deletion requests are distinct operations.
4. Remove app-held connection credentials and requested data according to the verified scope and applicable retention rules. Review uploaded objects, drafts, public album pages, publication receipts, account records and provider/back-up copies separately.
5. Confirm the result and any lawful retention exceptions to the requester. Deleting app records does not automatically remove already-published social posts. Those posts may need to be removed using the social platform's controls.

Meta Basic Settings currently offers a Data deletion instructions URL. Once these instructions are operational, publish a public HTTPS app page and use that URL. Do not enter a GitHub draft, placeholder page or nonfunctional automated callback.
